# Style sheet

A style sheet is per **document**, not per project. It records the decisions a style guide cannot make
in advance.

> "style sheet: a detailed record of the variable spellings, hyphenation, capitalisation, etc used in a
> particular document."
> — Chartered Institute of Editing and Proofreading, glossary

The distinction is institutional, not pedantic: CIEP lists *preparing a style guide* as explicitly not a
copyediting task. Authoring rules and recording choices are different jobs. `base.md` is the guide; this
is the sheet.

Keep it in the same place as the document. Fill it while editing at L3, not afterwards.
